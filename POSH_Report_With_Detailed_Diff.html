Appendix 3 ……………………………….……………………………………………………13 General Principles Sberbank Branch in India (for the sake of brevity, hereinafter referred to as “Bank”) is an equal employment opportunity provider and is committed to creating a healthy working environment that enables employees to work without fear of prejudice, gender bias and sexual harassment. The Bank seriously believes that all the employees of the Bank have the right to be treated with dignity, irrespective of their gender or hierarchy and the Bank mandates responsible conduct and dignified behavior to be exhibited on the part of all employees of the Bank across all levels. Providing for a safe and congenial work environment is an integral part of the Bank’s values and’s employment policy. 1.2 Through this gender-neutral POSH Policy, the Bank exhibits its commitment to enabling work conditions and a workplace that is gender sensitive, thereby recognizing role of every employee notwithstanding the gender, as an equal player. This POSH Policy ensures to follow the enactment of Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition & Redressal) Act, 2013 in letter and spirit. Sexual harassment in any form at the work place or during the course of the employment, if involves the Bank’s employees, shall be construed to be a grave offence and is therefore punishable. Through this POSH Policy, Bank’s endeavour is to set up an effective system in place which will provide prompt, unbiased and structured redressal of complaints received from the victims of sexual harassment to whom this POSH Policy applies to. Sexual harassment would mean and include the following, jointly or severally: Any unwelcome behavior with sexual undertones, which has been directed against a person either directly or indirectly. The unwelcome behavior could be in the form of: physical contact and advances/ offensive language; demand or request for sexual favours; sexually coloured remarks or/and bawdy humour; showing any pornographic material; any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature. Any unwelcome sexual advances, requests or demand for sexual favours, explicitly or implicitly, in return for either employment, promotion, examination or evaluation of a person towards any activity of the Bank. Any unwelcome sexual advances involving verbal, non-verbal, or physical conduct such as sexually coloured remarks, jokes, letters, phone calls, e-mail, WhatsApp, social media, SMS, gestures, showing pornography, lurid stares, physical contact or molestation, stalking, sounds, display of pictures, signs, verbal or non-verbal communication which offends the individual’s sensibilities and affects her/his performance. Entry into specific areas earmarked for female employees, with the intent to commit mischief and harassment. Eve teasing, innuendos and taunts, physical confinement against one’s will and intrusion into one’s privacy. Act or conduct by a person in authority, which creates a hostile environment at the workplace or intimidates the person belonging to the other sex. Aimlessly staring at a woman for a long period of time. The following circumstances, among other circumstances, if it occurs or is present in relation to or connected with any act or behaviour of sexual harassment may amount to sexual harassment: implied or explicit promise of preferential treatment in employment; or implied or explicit threat of detrimental treatment in employment; or implied or explicit threat about present or future employment status; or interference with work or creating an intimidating or offensive or hostile work environment for her; or humiliating treatment likely to affect health or safety. Sexual harassment cases can be classified into two categories: Quid pro quo (meaning this for that): Under the quid pro quo form of harassment, a person or authority, usually the superior of the victim, demands sexual favors for getting or keeping a job benefit and threatens to fire the employee if the conditions are not met. Creation of a hostile working environment: A hostile work environment arises when a co- worker or supervisor creates a work environment through verbal or physical conduct that interferes with a co-worker’s job performance or creates a workplace atmosphere, which is intimidating, hostile, offensive or humiliating, and an attack on personal dignity. Sexual harassment and abuse damage both, individual and Organizational Health. The Bank reiterates its commitment to providing its employees, a workplace free from harassment/ discrimination and where every employee is treated with dignity and respect. Responsibilities regarding Sexual Harassment All employees of the Bank have a personal responsibility to ensure that their behavior is not contrary to this POSH Policy. All employees are encouraged to reinforce the maintenance of a work environment free from sexual harassment. All employees of the Bank shall have to be clear that any form of sexual harassment in the Workplace is totally unacceptable. Each Employee must also fully understand the importance of the following matters in the Workplace, etc. for the prevention and elimination of Sexual Harassment: Have mutual respect for the rights of other people; Be aware that everyone has the right to equality regardless of difference in gender; Do not act upon one’s view of another person as the object of sexual interest in a manner that is unwelcome to that person; Eliminate the view of the distinction of gender roles in connection with one’s work; Give full consideration to the dignity of others, regardless of their sexual orientation or gender identity; Have a specific understanding of behavior and remarks that constitute sexual harassment. It is pertinent to understand that the question of whether certain behavior or remarks constitutes sexual harassment depends on how the person subjected to such behavior or remarks perceives or judges such behavior or remarks, regardless of the intention or awareness of the agent of such behavior or remarks. All employees of the Bank should make themselves aware of the following: builds an environment where all employees of the Branch report to the heads of structural units and the Head of the Compliance and Regulatory Oversight Division any violations of laws, internal regulatory and organizational-administrative documents of the Branch, standards of self-regulatory organizations, and professional ethics standards. Even if a certain behavior or remark is intended to express familiarity, there are cases where that may cause discomfort to the other person; There are differences in how different people perceive behavior and remarks of a sexual nature, and what makes different people feel uncomfortable; One must not arbitrarily speculate that another person will probably consent to or tolerate a certain behavior or remark; One must not arbitrarily assume that you have formed a good personal relationship with another person, which would justify a certain behavior or remark. Every employee of the Bank shall make themselves aware that another person may not always clearly express his or her intention, such as when such person is unable to reject Sexual Harassment by that employee in light of factors such as interpersonal relationships in the Bank (i.e. Workplace). Further, an employee must not repeat the same behavior or remark if he or she knows the other person has refused or dislikes that behavior or remark in the past. APPROVED BY Resolution of the HPLBC, Sberbank Branch in India dated 30/01/2025 No.181 POSH POLICY VERSION 2 IRD Details GENERAL PRINCIPLES Sberbank Branch in India (for the sake of brevity, hereinafter referred to as “Bank”) is an equal employment opportunity provider and is committed to creating a healthy working environment that enables employees to work without fear of prejudice, gender bias and sexual harassment. The Bank seriously believes that all the employees of the Bank have the right to be treated with dignity, irrespective of their gender or hierarchy and the Bank mandates responsible conduct and dignified behavior to be exhibited on the part of all employees of the Bank across all levels. Providing for a safe and congenial work environment is an integral part of the Bank’s values and’s employment policy. Through this gender-neutral POSH Policy, the Bank exhibits its commitment to enabling work conditions and a workplace that is gender sensitive, thereby recognizing role of every employee notwithstanding the gender, as an equal player. This POSH Policy ensures to follow the enactment of Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition & Redressal) Act, 2013 in letter and spirit. Sexual harassment in any form at the work place or during the course of the employment, if involves the Bank’s employees, shall be construed to be a grave offence and is therefore punishable. Through this POSH Policy, Bank’s endeavor is to set up an effective system in place which will provide prompt, unbiased and structured redressal of complaints received from the victims of sexual harassment to whom this POSH Policy applies to. Sexual harassment would mean and include the following, jointly or severally: Any unwelcome behavior with sexual undertones, which has been directed against a person either directly or indirectly. The unwelcome behavior could be in the form of: physical contact and advances/ offensive language; demand or request for sexual favours; sexually coloured remarks or/and bawdy humour; showing any pornographic material; any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature. Any unwelcome sexual advances, requests or demand for sexual favours, explicitly or implicitly, in return for either employment, promotion, examination or evaluation of a person towards any activity of the Bank. Any unwelcome sexual advances involving verbal, non-verbal, or physical conduct such as sexually coloured remarks, jokes, letters, phone calls, e-mail, WhatsApp, social media, SMS, gestures, showing pornography, lurid stares, physical contact or molestation, stalking, sounds, display of pictures, signs, verbal or non-verbal communication which offends the individual’s sensibilities and affects her/his performance. Entry into specific areas earmarked for female employees, with the intent to commit mischief and harassment. Eve teasing, innuendos and taunts, physical confinement against one’s will and intrusion into one’s privacy. Act or conduct by a person in authority, which creates a hostile environment at the workplace or intimidates the person belonging to the other sex. Aimlessly staring at a woman for a long period of time. The following circumstances, among other circumstances, if it occurs or is present in relation to or connected with any act or behaviour of sexual harassment may amount to sexual harassment: implied or explicit promise of preferential treatment in employment; or implied or explicit threat of detrimental treatment in employment; or implied or explicit threat about present or future employment status; or interference with work or creating an intimidating or offensive or hostile work environment for her; or humiliating treatment likely to affect health or safety. Sexual harassment cases can be classified into two categories: Quid pro quo (meaning this for that): Under the quid pro quo form of harassment, a person or authority, usually the superior of the victim, demands sexual favors for getting or keeping a job benefit and threatens to fire the employee if the conditions are not met. Creation of a hostile working environment: A hostile work environment arises when a co- worker or supervisor creates a work environment through verbal or physical conduct that interferes with a co-worker’s job performance or creates a workplace atmosphere, which is intimidating, hostile, offensive or humiliating, and an attack on personal dignity. Sexual harassment and abuse damage both, individual and Organizational Health. The Bank reiterates its commitment to providing its employees, a workplace free from harassment/ discrimination and where every employee is treated with dignity and respect. THE SEXUAL HARASSMENT OF WOMEN AT WORKPLACE (PREVENTION, PROHIBITION AND REDRESSAL) ACT, 2013 ACT NO. 14 OF 2013 [22nd April, 2013] An Act to provide protection against sexual harassment of women at workplace and for the prevention and redressal of complaints of sexual harassment and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.
